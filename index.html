Welcome to Icebreaker Village!

Everyone here gets their own square patch of land. Your job: make it yours, and use it to tell everyone a little about you.

How to move in

  1. Check out today's branch (your host will share the name) and git pull.
  2. Open your AI coding agent in this repo and tell it:
    Read AGENTS.md and add me to Icebreaker Village.
  3. Answer its questions (all in one go), let it work for a minute or two, then refresh this page to see your patch.
